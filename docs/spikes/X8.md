# Spike X8: passenger seat and the ARC device

Question (D12, C7): where does the guest sit, and can the ARC device move so both players reach it?

Scenario `carseat` (`test/PacificTogetherTest/Scripts/scenarios/carseat.lua`, helpers in `scenarios/carlib.lua`), run
without `-Headless` for screenshots:

```powershell
tools\run-test.ps1 -Scenario carseat -Root C:\PTLanes\laneD -Port 7841 -Seconds 420
tools\run-test.ps1 -Scenario carseat -Root C:\PTLanes\laneD -Port 7841 -Seconds 420 -Mutate noarc   # must FAIL
```

The host turns the car to yaw 0 (so world-aligned bounds are car-local boxes), sits in the driver's seat, seats the
guest at the passenger seat, then both sides log the ARC's layout, look forward and at the ARC (screenshot each), move
the ARC between the seats, repeat, and finally press interact on what the game highlights.

Runs (all in `local/test-results/`, lane D): `20261019-191154-laneD-carseat` (first probe), `-192507-` (26/31),
`-193105-` (27/31), `-193711-` (29/31), `20261019-200234-laneD-carseat` (**31/31**, final), mutation
`20261020-113734-laneD-carseat-noarc` (headless), and `20261020-114007-laneD-carseat` (31/31 headless, after the
`carlib.lua` changes of X7). Coordinates are car-local uu (X forward, Y right, Z up).

## Observations

- `NetEmulation.PktLag 0` on the host: engine log `Command not recognized: NetEmulation.PktLag 0` (run 191154). The
  exe has no `NetEmulation` string at all (ASCII or UTF-16); only the `FPacketSimulationSettings` property names.
- The ARC device is two actors, both `ABP_Manifestation_CarEquipment_Internal_C`:
  `BP_Carpart_Internal_Gobbler_C` (anchor tube and base, `Highlight`, inventory `BP_InventoryContainerComponent_ExternalInventory`)
  and `BP_Carpart_Internal_GobblerScreen_C` (map screen on a swivel arm, `ScreenHighlight`, `BP_InteractCameraComponent`,
  `UMG Gobbler Screen`). Role 3 on both sides: each side has its own pair on its own copy of the car (also on the
  guest's copy of the replicated car).
- They hang on `USlotSceneComponent`s under the car's `Interior` mesh: the gobbler on `NODE_AddSlotSceneComponent-1`
  (not on the car's `SLOT_Internal_Gobbler`, which is also there and empty), the screen on `SLOT_Internal_GobblerScreen`.
  Slots and actors sit at the car origin with relative location 0; the meshes are modelled in car space.
- Boxes (run 200234, yaw 0, mesh bounds; skeletal mesh bounds are left out, they cover 106 x 68 x 110 uu):
  gobbler highlight mesh centre (56.3, 42.5, 0.6), half size (23.2, 33.1, 7.0), i.e. on the passenger seat cushion;
  gobbler `MainMesh` (112.4, 43.7, -15.4) in the passenger footwell; screen highlight mesh (76.6, 23.1, 40.3) on the
  host and (80.1, 35.9, 39.9) on the guest: the screen swivels by local state.
- Seats: `GetCarAttachTransformForCharacter` (the game's seat for a character, called from Lua with an out table)
  gives the driver's seat (44.0, -45.8, -12.3); `TempSocket_Seat` (26.6, -45.8, -15.6); the in-car camera
  `Socket_Camera` (44.7, -45.8, 62.0); gear shifter `Socket_GearBox` (103.2, 0, -38.1); car computer (115.1, 0, 27.3).
  Mirroring the driver's seat across Y gives the passenger seat (46.2, 45.8, -12.3). The host attaches the guest there
  (movement off, collision off, `K2_AttachToActor` KeepWorld); the guest receives `AttachmentReplication` offset
  (46.2, 45.8, -12.3) and its camera is at (45.1, 46.8, 62.0), mirror of the driver's camera.
- Moving: `K2_SetRelativeLocation` on both slots by (0, -42.5, 0) (each side moves its own pair) moves both actors and
  every mesh by exactly that; the gobbler highlight box is then centred on Y = 0 (Y -33 .. 33).
- Checks in run 200234, both sides: `arc_found`, `arc_blocks_passenger_seat` (before: the passenger body core, seat
  point to eye height, 10 uu around, cuts into the gobbler highlight mesh), `arc_moved` (within 2 uu of the wanted
  spot), `passenger_seat_clear` (after), `highlights_arc` (the game's `BP_HighlightManager_C.CurrentHighlight` is the
  screen's `ScreenHighlight` while looking at the moved ARC), `uses_arc` (after `HighlightPress(true/false)` the
  screen's `BP_InteractCameraComponent."Focused Player"` is our own controller).
- Screenshots, run 200234 (`<role>-HighresScreenshotNNNNN.png`, order = tags `before-forward, before-arc,
  after-forward, after-arc, after-use`, numbers 00017-00021):
  host 00018 (ARC on the passenger seat seen from the driver's seat), host 00019 (forward view after the move, clear),
  host 00020 (ARC between the seats, the screen seen from its edge), host 00021 (map view after interact);
  client 00018 (before: the screen 35 uu in front of the guest's face), client 00020 (after: the screen faces the
  guest between the seats), client 00021 (map view, empty map). Run 192507: host 00005 (driving camera, possessed car,
  ARC moved: screen at the right of the windshield), client 00006 (guest map view).
- The possessed car's camera did not turn: `SetControlRotation`, writing `CameraControllerRotation_Target`, and
  calling `LookLeftRight`/`LookUpDown` every frame all left `camRot` at the car's rotation (runs 192507, 193105,
  193711; limits `CameraYawMinMaxAngles` (-160, 140), pitch (-50, 60)). The final run therefore seats the host as a
  character in the driver's seat (its camera at (42.4, -45.4, 62.0), within 3 uu of `Socket_Camera`). In run 191154
  the possessed camera happened to face the passenger seat (pitch -32, +59 deg from forward) and the host's highlight
  was the ARC screen there.
- `CommandEnterCar(true, nil, false)` on the host's character returned without error and did not seat it within 6 s
  in all five runs; `Possess(car)` worked (as in the reference).
- The guest's view in the car is covered by brown blotches (client 00017-00020, every run, both seat positions); the
  host's view from the same kind of character camera in the driver's seat has none (host 00017-00020), and the guest's
  view outside the car in X13 had none. No post-process component in the world has weight > 0 except the
  time-of-day ones, on both sides (`PT|pp` lines, run 193711).
- On the guest the replicated car's physics is unusable: with simulation on, the car left the garage (e.g. at
  (29425, -2894, 8855) then (55720, 19074, 22461) within seconds, run 191154) while the attached guest stayed behind.
  The final runs make it kinematic and place it every frame (X7).
- Mutation `noarc` (ARC not moved, run 113734): `arc_moved` and `passenger_seat_clear` FAIL on both sides (27/31);
  `highlights_arc` and `uses_arc` still pass (the ARC on the passenger seat is also highlighted and used from both
  seats). Those two failed for real in runs 192507 and 193105 (camera not facing the ARC), so they can fail.
- Headless (`-nullrhi`) run 114007 passes all 31 checks too: highlight and interact do not need rendering.

## Derived

- The ARC can move: its two actors follow their slot components, and moving the slots by (0, -42.5, 0) puts the
  device between the seats with the passenger's body core clear of it, while the game's own highlight and interact
  still select and open the screen from both seats. Nothing about the ARC is replicated, so each side moves its own.
- The game already turns the screen to its local player, so after the move the host's screen faces the host's state and
  the guest's copy faces the guest.
- Using the game's own seat transform (`GetCarAttachTransformForCharacter`) mirrored on Y gives a passenger seat
  that matches the driver's seat height and the driver's camera height exactly.

## Guesses

- The blotches are a screen effect the game applies to a character camera in the car when that character is not the
  one the game thinks is driving (e.g. a material parameter collection or a camera post-process material set by
  `PDP_MainCharacter_C` in-car state); the guest never entered the car through the game.
- The guest's empty ARC map is its own minimap state (no route, no map export on the guest).
- When the gobbler is reinstalled (part swap), the new manifestation attaches to the moved slot and stays moved.

## Unknowns

- Whether the moved slots survive a part swap, a save/load, or a car rebuild (not tested).
- Whether the possessed car camera can be turned from Lua (input path), and whether the screen faces the driver when
  the host really drives (run 192507 suggests it does).
- Cause of the guest's blotch overlay.
- Whether the guest's dashboard instruments are complete (client 00019 shows the hood and engine where the host sees
  the dashboard).

## Verdicts

- Where is the ARC device: `BP_Carpart_Internal_Gobbler_C` + `BP_Carpart_Internal_GobblerScreen_C` on two slot
  components of the car's interior, on the passenger seat. **Found.**
- Can it move: yes, by the slots' relative location, per side; between the seats both players highlight and use it
  (31/31). **Keep D12.**
- Guest in the passenger seat with the game's seat logic: yes, mirrored `GetCarAttachTransformForCharacter`.
  **Works for seating; entering through the game's own door is M3 work.**
