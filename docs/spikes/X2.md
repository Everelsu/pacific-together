# Spike X2: actor identity, replication and loot

> Historical research; see the [index](README.md) for current implementation and later evidence.

October 19-20, 2026, lane B. Historical experiment; shipping loot synchronization came later in M4.
Scenarios `x2garage`/`x2zone`, pair `C:\PTLanes\laneB`, port 7821. The zone was opened directly rather than through a run.
Both players were placed near each target to control relevancy. Evidence: `local/test-results/<run>`.

## Results and test corrections

Final runs: `20261020-134215-laneB-x2garage` **32/32**, `20261020-133801-laneB-x2zone` **33/33**.
The scenario diffs class/path/position/loaded/role/pending-kill lists and logs interaction hooks.

- **Pending kill matters:** `IsValid` remained true until GC, 17.5-33 s after destroy. Check
  `HasAnyInternalFlags(0x20000000)` and filter dead actors from FindAllOf. Earlier 8-40 s removal measurements were GC delays.
- A removal assertion must first see the actor and finish before moving to another target; otherwise no replication
  or loss of relevancy can falsely pass it. Each target must be distinct.
- Taking/pickup checks invoked functions directly. They prove those calls work, not that the inventory UI/input path works.
  The only consistently observed player input chain was opening a container.

## Observations

### Identity

- All 59 loaded garage actors matched by full path and position. Runtime actors mostly matched position under different
  names; all 14 garage loot-like actors were spawned. Cooked runtime suffixes count down from 2^31 across classes.
- In the zone, 181 loaded actors matched. Three preplaced structures were alive only on the guest because the host
  destroyed them. Pending-kill entries had initially made the stable-ID check fail.
- Each process independently chose prefab loot variants and contents. Even same-name/same-position containers differed:
  host Fabric/Flare versus guest Fabric x3 in `114921`. Runtime names are not stable IDs.
- Host-only zone actors included barrels, wrecks/parts, lockboxes and world items. A client-only movement base produced
  unresolved-NetGUID warnings on the host (`204004`).

### Replication

- `SetReplicates`, `SetReplicateMovement`, `ForceNetUpdate` showed host actors within four seconds.
- A loaded garage rock bound to the guest's same-path copy (Role 1); a zone road ending did not bind within 20 s.
- Spawned actors arrived as additional actors, leaving guest-local copies alive. Their BeginPlay rolled independent loot.
- Actors attached to nonreplicated parents arrived at 0,0,0. Detaching first fixed position in all but one recorded run.
- Host destruction or the game's pickup removed the replicated copy in 0.1-1 s. Relevancy also removes copies, so
  absence is not evidence of a pickup and must not award loot.
- A gas-pump-handle copy ran its own disabling/destruction path; its survival was not rechecked with PendingKill.

### Interaction

- Container opening: `Command Interact` -> `Interact_WorldInteraction` -> `HighlightPress` ->
  `InteractPressed/Released` -> lootable `OnInteractedWith`, roll/open visual updates, `OpenInventoryContainer`.
  Some runs forced the highlight rather than establishing a natural trace.
- `ItemSlot:TransferStackIntoContainer` moved loot and fired count-change events. No UI drag/click transfer was exercised.
- World pickup used `Interaction_InventoryAction` / `LongInventoryInteract`; a 600 ms input hold succeeded in only
  2/43 runs (`201418`, `133801`), both to hands. Save-less inventory setup may matter; not established.
- Direct `Pickup Actor(item, false)` took ordinary items into the backpack; balls required `To Hands=true`, even with
  an empty backpack. The assertion required the world item gone and carried count increased before explicit cleanup.
- Highlight durations observed: lootable long 0.51 s; world-item short 0.32 s, long 0.38 s. Scrap and anchors were not driven.

## Derived design

Use paths for loaded actors and host-assigned class/initial-position/ordinal identities for runtime actors. Suppress
client-local duplicates, mirror host existence/position, and send explicit authoritative state and interaction intents.
Actor replication alone does not synchronize loot contents or Blueprint side effects. The later implementation lives
in `ids.lua`, `loot.lua` and the M4 reports; these early proposals are not its wire specification.

## Unknowns at the time

- Why the zone road ending failed to bind, and why natural pickup rarely completed in the save-less fixture.
- The real inventory UI transfer path, ongoing guest spawner behavior and gas-pump-handle lifetime.
- Scrap/anchor paths were dump-only findings: `BP_ItemHarvestableComponent`, `DoSmash/DoCut/DoStrip`,
  `Long Grab Complete` -> `AnchorTaken`, `Inventory_AnchorStorage` and `DepositAnchorsAfterRun`.
- Garage inventory was identified as manager-owned; later work corrected its field to `Inventories_Garage`.
- Whether a K2_DestroyActor hook interfered with behavior was not established; the early experiments measured GC.
