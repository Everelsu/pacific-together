# Spike X7: the car on the guest at 0, 100 and 150 ms lag

Question (C1, C2): with the host driving and the guest riding, how far is the guest's car from the host's, how should
the guest interpolate, and what about the guest's own duplicate car?

Scenario `carride` (`test/PacificTogetherTest/Scripts/scenarios/carride.lua`, helpers in `scenarios/carlib.lua`), run
through `tools/run-car.ps1`, which starts the lag relay `tools/udp-lag.ps1` and afterwards `tools/car-error.ps1`:

```powershell
tools\run-car.ps1 -Root C:\PTLanes\laneD -Port 7841 -Headless -ResultsDir <repo>\local\test-results
tools\run-car.ps1 ... -Mutate freeze     # guest never places its kinematic car: motion "smooth" checks must FAIL
tools\run-car.ps1 ... -Mutate nodedupe   # guest keeps its own car copy: client_one_car must FAIL
```

The host listens in the garage, spawns and possesses a character for the guest, possesses the car (bot throttle
forward/back every 4 s, steer 0.3, put back at its start when stuck for 3 s), seats the guest at the passenger seat
(X8) and logs its car every frame (`PT|carh`). The guest destroys its own car copy as soon as it is in the garage,
then runs 12 phases of 18 s: `phys` (engine physics on the replicated car), `snap`, `interp`, `motion`, each at 0, 100
and 150 ms one-way lag, logging its car every frame (`PT|carc`) and every new `ReplicatedMovement` (`PT|crep`).
`car-error.ps1` lines the two logs up on the UE4SS log time stamps (system clock) and reports per phase: error at the
same time, the best constant delay and the error left after it (residual), speed jitter, stalls, snapshot gaps, ping.
2 s after each phase change, and each host frame over 100 ms plus the 1.5 s after it (see Observations), are skipped;
a motion phase with less than half its frames left fails.

Check `smooth` (motion phases): the car drove (> 150 uu/s), no frozen frames (< 1 %), the guest's path within 10 uu
p95 of the host's, compared per 2 s window against that window's best delay (`localResidualP95`), frame-to-frame speed
no more uneven than 2 x the host's own picture + 5 points, delay <= lag + 300 ms. Check `rtt`: the host's view of the
guest's ping rose by 2 x lag (+-30 ms) over the lag-0 phases.

Runs (all in `local/test-results/`, lane D): `20261019-194435-laneD-carride` (first), `-195752-`, `-201151-`, `-202125-`,
`20261019-202851-laneD-carride` (all car checks pass; `client_one_car` failed, fixed after), `-204735-` (24/24, but the
car stuck against a wall at lag 150), `20261019-205607-laneD-carride` (machine loaded, host 15-22 fps: two motion
checks failed), `20261020-110239-` and `-111206-` (motion at lag 100 failed, see below),
**`20261020-112130-laneD-carride` (final: 25/25 and all 11 car checks pass)**, mutations
`20261020-112826-laneD-carride-freeze` and `20261020-113456-laneD-carride-nodedupe`. `car-error.txt` in each folder
holds the analysis by the final `car-error.ps1` (re-run on the old logs; `summary.txt` has one block appended per
analysis run).

## Derived

- The guest must not simulate the host's car as things stand: physics on its car is wrong by hundreds of uu.
  Kinematic chassis, vehicle controller tick off, placed by us every frame (the reference's finding, confirmed).
- What the guest sees lags the host by about one-way lag + render delay (120-140 ms at lag 0, 230-240 at 100 ms,
  ~290 at 150 ms). Against that delay the error left is a few uu: the guest sees the host's path, late.
- Snapping is accurate but stutters (a frozen frame in four to seven). Interpolating between snapshots on arrival
  times removes the stalls but keeps the network's timing noise (guest jitter 30-39 % vs host 20-25 %). Stamping each
  snapshot with its "motion time" (distance / replicated speed) makes the guest's car as even as the host's own picture.
- The adaptive render delay must start at its target when the mode starts; ramping from 100 ms smears the first seconds
  (fixed in `carride.lua` after run 205607). After that it drifts by 20-80 ms within a phase, which moves the car
  along its path by a few uu per second, not frame to frame; the smooth check compares per 2 s window for that reason.
- When the host's frames get long (100+ ms) its car slows down in real time (the physics step is capped) while the
  guest's motion clock plays the car's own simulated time evenly; the "error" there is the host's hesitation, which
  the guest does not copy. When the gaps reach the render delay (140+ ms host frames) the guest also extrapolates and
  corrects by up to ~60 uu. Only a heavily loaded host (15-22 fps, run 205607) failed the check after both.

## Guesses

- Real internet jitter (not the relay's fixed delay) will push the adaptive delay up to its 250 ms cap more often; the
  car will then trail by lag + 250 ms, still smooth.
- The replicated car is thrown on arrival by the guest's own character capsule, whose collision is still on until the
  guest turns it off (`L.ride`); run 111206's good `phys` phase would be a run where that did not happen. If so,
  engine physics replication with the passenger's collision off from the start could be as smooth as `motion` with
  almost no delay.
- The car's sounds, wheels and suspension animation on the guest may need the vehicle controller's state, which is
  off while kinematic (not looked at).
- The destroyed duplicate car stays until garbage collection; hiding it and its parts is enough for M3.

## Unknowns

- Why `phys` worked once (run 111206) and never otherwise.
- Behaviour at real driving speeds (the garage yard limits the bot to ~13 km/h), over long drives and zone streaming.
- Packet loss (the relay only delays).
- What `CommandEnterCar` needs to work from Lua (C1: entering through the game's door interaction).
- The guest's camera inside the moving car (attached to the car; not measured).

## Verdicts

- Guest-side car position error versus the host, per frame, at 0 / 100 / 150 ms: **logged**; the same-time error
  grows with lag (mean 52 / 84 / 106 uu at ~13 km/h, run 112130); after the delay a few uu are left.
- Interpolation design: **motion-time Hermite interpolation** (below) passes `smooth` at all three lags (run 112130,
  and on the logs of 202125, 202851, 110239, 111206, 113456); it failed at lag 100 on a host at 15-22 fps (run
  205607). **Keep the stock
  replicated car transform, kinematic car on the guest, interpolate there.** Engine physics on the guest's car: no,
  unless the Guess above is confirmed.
- Duplicate local car on the guest: **exists, at the same spot; destroy it (and its car-part actors) on arrival**, and
  treat a car at exactly (0,0,0) as gone.

## Recommended M3 design (C1-C7)

- **C2 car on the guest** (`carlib.lua` `L.follow`, mode `motion`): chassis `SetSimulatePhysics(false)`,
  `VehicleController` tick off. Every frame read `ReplicatedMovement`; on a new value record a snapshot with arrival
  time `t` and motion time `tm = prev.tm + clamp(distance / mean replicated speed, 0.3..3 x arrival gap)`, pulled 10 %
  towards `t`. Render at `now - delay` by cubic Hermite between the two snapshots around it using their replicated
  velocities, rotation by shortest-path lerp; extrapolate at most 250 ms. `delay = clamp(1.5 x p95 of the last 60
  snapshot gaps + 20 ms, 100, 250 ms)`, starting at that value and moving 1 % per frame after. To add in M3: blend a
  correction after extrapolation over a few frames instead of jumping, and let the cap grow when the gaps do (host
  stutter). The guest's character stays attached to the car, so the passenger rides along for free.
- **C1 seats**: host drives with `Possess(car)` until the game's door interaction is understood; the guest's door
  intent makes the host seat it at the mirrored driver's seat (X8) and attach it (movement and collision off on both
  sides); exit = detach, collision and movement on.
- **Duplicate car**: on every level load the guest destroys its own Role-3 `BaseNewCar` and the car-part actors
  attached to it at once, then points the game's `NewCarActor` reference at the replicated car.
- **C7**: per X8: guest in the mirrored seat, both sides move the ARC slots by (0, -42.5, 0) locally.
- **C3-C6** (visible state, health, repairs, trunk): not covered by this spike. Only the car's transform replicates,
  so they are host state broadcasts and guest intents as in the plan.
